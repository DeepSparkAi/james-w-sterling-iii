About

Classical questions, stated in modern terms.

James W. Sterling, III writes about metaphysics and causation, with particular attention to Aquinas's first-mover argument and modern attempts to replace a non-derivative source with another physical event, object, field, fluctuation, process, or prior state.

The work focuses on explanatory structure: what follows when every proposed physical source remains inside the physical description whose sufficiency is itself under examination?
